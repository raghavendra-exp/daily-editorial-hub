🏛️ Exam Knowledge Vault & Static Study Guides

High-yield reference guides for Constitutional Articles, Landmark Judgments, and Economic Terms.

Part III: Fundamental Rights

Articles 14, 19, 21 — The Golden Triangle

Article 14: Equality before law & equal protection.
Article 19: Six democratic freedoms (speech, assembly, movement).
Article 21: Right to Life and Personal Liberty (expanded to include privacy, clean environment, speedy trial).

Constitutional Remedies

Article 32 & Article 226 — Writs

The "Heart and Soul of the Constitution" (Dr. B.R. Ambedkar). Empowering SC and High Courts to issue writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo-Warranto.

Federalism & Governance

Article 123 & Article 213 — Ordinances

Executive lawmaking power during legislative recess. Subject to judicial review; re-promulgation without legislative debate is a fraud on the Constitution (D.C. Wadhwa case).

Judicial Powers

Article 142 — Complete Justice

Inherent power of Supreme Court to pass any decree necessary for doing complete justice in any cause or matter pending before it.

Fiscal Federalism

Article 280 — Finance Commission

Quinquennial body recommending net tax devolution between Centre and States and horizontal distribution principles among States.

Emergency Provisions

Article 356 — President's Rule

Imposition on breakdown of constitutional machinery in states. Strictly circumscribed by S.R. Bommai v. Union of India (1994).

Landmark SC Judgment (1973)

Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala

Established the Basic Structure Doctrine. Parliament has vast amending powers under Article 368 but cannot alter the basic features (democracy, federalism, judicial review, rule of law).

Privacy & Fundamental Rights (2017)

K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India

Unanimous 9-judge bench ruled that the Right to Privacy is a Fundamental Right under Article 21. Led directly to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

Personal Liberty & Procedure (1978)

Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India

Interpreted 'procedure established by law' as equivalent to 'due process of law'—procedure must be just, fair, and reasonable, not arbitrary.

Anti-Defection (Tenth Schedule)

Kihoto Hollohan v. Zachillhu (1992)

Upheld the Speaker's power under Tenth Schedule but established that the Speaker acts as a tribunal, making their orders subject to judicial review for mala fides.

Transparency & Governance

Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005

Mandatory proactive disclosures under Section 4(1)(b), 30-day response window, and independent Central/State Information Commissions ensuring participatory democracy.

Monetary Policy Tool

Policy Repo Rate & Reverse Repo

Repo Rate: Rate at which RBI lends short-term liquidity to commercial banks against government securities.
Reverse Repo: Rate at which RBI absorbs liquidity from banks.

Liquidity Framework

Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) & MSF

SDF: Floor of the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) corridor without collateral requirement.
Marginal Standing Facility (MSF): Penal emergency borrowing rate (ceiling of corridor).

Reserve Ratios

Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) & SLR

CRR: Percentage of Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL) banks must park as cash with RBI (earns no interest).
SLR: Percentage maintained in liquid assets (gold, approved G-Secs).

Inflation Targeting

Headline Inflation vs. Core Inflation

Headline Inflation: Comprehensive measure (CPI-Combined) including volatile food and fuel prices.
Core Inflation: CPI excluding food and fuel, indicating underlying demand pressures.

External Sector

Current Account Deficit (CAD) vs. Fiscal Deficit

CAD: Difference between foreign currency inflows and outflows from trade in goods, services, and transfers.
Fiscal Deficit: Excess of total government expenditure over non-debt receipts.
